    OUR HERITAGE

    At House of Iyengars, we bring the timeless flavours of traditional Iyengar homes to your doorstep.

    Our cloud kitchen follows the culinary traditions passed down through generations — simple, wholesome ingredients, patient preparation and food made fresh with love and devotion.

    We believe in abundance and generosity. Every offering is prepared with care, enriched with generous portions of dry fruits, and made with an unwavering commitment to hygiene and nutrition.

    True to our tradition, we use NO ONION, NO GARLIC.

    From our signature Puliyodhare, Kadagore and Yellore to comforting Pongal, aromatic Vaangibath, refreshing Curd Rice and divine Hayagreeva, every dish is prepared the traditional way, preserving the flavours and memories of our heritage.

    For us, this is more than cooking. It is about preserving a legacy, celebrating our roots and sharing the goodness of our ancestral kitchens with every home we serve.

    We consider this our higher calling.
